DRAGON’S RHYTHM

Privacy Policy

Dragon’s Rhythm is designed to keep your personal health and fitness data on your device. No account is required, DragonBuilds does not receive the data you enter into the app, and Coach and Insights do not send your data to an external AI or cloud service.

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Overview

Dragon’s Rhythm is a local-first health and fitness app. You do not need to create an account or sign in to use it. DragonBuilds does not operate a server account containing your Dragon’s Rhythm records and does not receive the health, fitness, routine, or progress information you enter into the app.

In short: No login. No advertising. No analytics SDK. No cloud AI. Your app records stay on your device unless you choose to export or move them.

Data stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, Dragon’s Rhythm may store information such as medications and schedules, meals and nutrition, activities and workouts, hydration, sleep, weight and body measurements, habits, program progress, settings, and other check-in data.

This information is stored locally for the app to provide its tracking, planning, program, Coach, and Insights features. Dragon’s Rhythm does not transmit this app data to DragonBuilds.

Coach and Insights

Coach and Insights operate internally using data recorded in Dragon’s Rhythm. The app does not send your records, findings, coaching results, or questions to a third-party AI provider or external cloud-processing service.

Progress photos

Progress photos used by Dragon’s Rhythm are stored on the device. Dragon’s Rhythm does not upload progress photos to DragonBuilds. Your operating system or device backup settings may separately back up device or app data according to the services you have enabled.

Backups and exports

Dragon’s Rhythm can create backup or export files. The app does not restrict where you save those files. You may choose to keep them locally, move them to another device, save them to removable storage, send them through another app, or place them in a cloud-storage service.

Once you choose to move or save a backup outside Dragon’s Rhythm, that copy is handled by the destination you selected and is subject to that service or provider’s privacy and security practices. DragonBuilds does not receive your backup merely because you created or exported it.

Youth Mode

Youth Mode can be used independently and follows the same local-first privacy model as the rest of Dragon’s Rhythm. No online account is required, and DragonBuilds does not receive the information entered or generated while Youth Mode is being used.

Dragon’s Rhythm does not use advertising, behavioral tracking, online profiling, or analytics SDKs in Youth Mode. Coach and Insights in Youth Mode also run internally rather than sending youth data to an outside AI service.

If Dragon’s Rhythm is used on a shared device, users should consider the device’s own lock-screen, app-access, backup, and account settings because anyone with access to the device may also be able to access locally stored app information.

DragonBuilds website privacy

The DragonBuilds website does not use advertising, analytics tracking, contact forms, mailing-list signup forms, or tracking cookies for Dragon’s Rhythm.

Like most websites, the hosting and network services that deliver the site may process ordinary technical request information such as an IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, and timestamps for delivery, reliability, and security. The site may also load static presentation resources, such as web fonts, from third-party content providers; those providers may receive standard network request information when those resources are requested.

Third-party services

Dragon’s Rhythm does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or cloud-AI SDKs that receive your health or fitness records. Services you independently choose to use outside the app—such as an operating-system backup service, cloud-storage provider, email app, or file-sharing service—operate under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Because DragonBuilds does not maintain a server-side Dragon’s Rhythm account or copy of your app records, there is no DragonBuilds account to close or remote app-data profile to delete. You can remove records within the app or uninstall the app to remove app-managed local data, subject to your device and operating system’s own backup and restore behavior.

Backup or export files you created must be deleted separately from every location where you chose to save or copy them. Copies held by a cloud or backup provider may remain until removed under that provider’s controls and retention rules.

Security

Keeping Dragon’s Rhythm data local reduces the amount of personal data transmitted to outside services, but the security of locally stored information also depends on your device. Device locks, operating-system updates, account security, backup settings, and control over exported files all affect the privacy of your records.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Dragon’s Rhythm changes. If the app later adds accounts, online synchronization, analytics, cloud processing, advertising, or another feature that materially changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated to describe that change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.
