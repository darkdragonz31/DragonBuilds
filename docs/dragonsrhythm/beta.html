A few quick answers

Is this for Android or iPhone?

This signup is for the Android beta distributed through Google Play. There is no iPhone beta being offered here.

What will it cost?

The planned public release will have a free version and a one-time $9.99 Full upgrade. The feature split and beta access details are still being finalized. Signing up does not make a purchase.

Do I have to share health records?

No. The signup only asks for contact information and optional device details. Please don’t send medication lists, health history, progress photos, or other sensitive information through this form.

How do I report a problem?

Use the Dragon’s Rhythm support form. Include your app version, phone model, what happened, and what you expected. Remove private information from screenshots.
