Support & beta feedback

Questions, bugs, and honest feedback are welcome. Your testing helps shape the first public release.

Email support

mygarage.support@gmail.com

Please include enough detail to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are especially helpful.

What to include

  • The MyGarage app version shown on the About screen.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • What you were trying to do.
  • What happened instead, including any message shown.
  • Whether the issue happens every time or only sometimes.

Beta feedback

Feedback does not need to be formal. Useful reports include confusing wording, a task that took too many taps, information that appeared in the wrong place, or a feature that did not behave the way you expected.

Before sending app data

Please avoid emailing sensitive personal information. Vehicle identification numbers, addresses, receipt details, and other private content may appear in screenshots or exports, so review attachments before sending them.

Privacy

For information about how MyGarage handles app data, read the MyGarage Privacy Policy.
