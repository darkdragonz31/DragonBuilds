MyGarage closed beta

Help test MyGarage before release.

I’m preparing the first Google Play closed test. If you use Android and want to put MyGarage through real-world use, sign up below.

What happens next

Simple signup. Real testing.

The first closed test will use Google Play. Testers will receive an invitation at the Google account they use with the Play Store.

This is not a big time commitment

Stay enrolled for 14 days. Test when you have time.

You do not need to use MyGarage every day, spend hours testing, or work through a giant checklist. A few minutes here and there using the app the way you naturally would is useful.

The important part: once you opt into the Google Play closed test, please remain opted in for the full testing period and wait for my all-clear before opting out. Staying enrolled does not mean you have to actively test every day.
